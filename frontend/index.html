      Why the weather matters

      Water plants make oxygen only in sunlight. After a cloudy day, on a calm, warm night, they use up oxygen and no wind mixes in fresh air, so oxygen is lowest just before sunrise. That is when fish die.

      Why there's no photo check

      We tested a computer model that guesses the disease from a fish photo. The public photos are mostly aquarium fish, not carps in ponds, and the model was wrong about one time in three. That is not safe enough for your fish, so the app does not do it.

            Terms & Privacy

            What this is

            AquaNexus is a free early-warning helper for small fish ponds, built by Team Orbit for DevHack 2026. It helps you decide what to check and when. It does not replace your fisheries officer, KVK or vet, and it cannot promise that your fish will be safe.

            Limits of the data

            The demo pond's readings are simulated, not from a real pond. Weather forecasts can be wrong. The disease guide shows possible causes, never a diagnosis. The AI assistant can make mistakes. Always confirm before you act.

            No chemical or medicine advice

            The app never suggests chemicals, medicines or amounts to add to the pond. Treatment must come from a fisheries officer or KVK.

            Privacy

            • No account and no sign-up.
            • The app does not ask for your location. Tonight's weather is always for Mangaluru. Your phone gets it straight from Open-Meteo, so Open-Meteo sees your internet address, as any website would.
            • Your language, theme, ticked steps, alert history and the last weather forecast are saved only in this browser. Clearing the browser's data removes them.
            • Test-kit numbers and the readings on screen go to our server to be checked, and are not saved there. Readings from a pond sensor stay in the server's memory only until it restarts.
            • Questions to the assistant are sent, with the readings on screen, to the AI service (now Google Gemini) to get an answer. Google's own terms apply to what it receives. Don't type your name, phone number or other personal details.
            • To keep the site working and stop misuse, our server and our host (Render) keep short technical records, such as which page was asked for and the internet address it came from.
            • No ads, no tracking, and nothing about you is sold.
