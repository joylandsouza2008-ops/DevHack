          Data sources

          The simulated pond uses these open datasets and public services. Only the Live sensor card shows readings sent by a pond sensor.

          • Pondsdata (Kaggle)

            Real sensor readings from 3 fish ponds in Andhra Pradesh (2022-23). Used to make the simulated demo pond and to test our oxygen predictions.

            Licence: unknown (not stated by the uploader).

          • Open-Meteo

            Tonight's real weather forecast for Mangaluru, for the night oxygen-crash warning.

            Licence: data CC BY 4.0. Free API, non-commercial use only.

          • FAO, TNAU, UF/IFAS, UGA Extension, UK Met Office

            Published guides and research papers behind the Safe / Warning / Danger limits and the “What to do now” steps.

            Copyright of each publisher. We cite their facts; we don't copy them.

          • Aquaculture Water Quality Dataset (Mendeley)

            Checked but not used: some values are impossible (e.g. water at 84 °C).

            Licence: CC BY 4.0.

          • Freshwater Fish Disease Aquaculture in South Asia (Kaggle)

            Fish disease photos for research only. Not used in the app.

            Licence: CC0 (public domain).
